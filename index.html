The Skew Question

Two dice, one very strange shape. Can a die like this really be fair? Roll, record, and decide.

The Dice Lab makes the Skew d6, a die with slanted, parallelogram-shaped faces, and advertises it as just as fair as a regular die. Next to it is an ordinary cube. Your team can roll either die as many times as you choose, up to 10,000 rolls each.

Is either die unfair?

You decide how much evidence is enough, and you explain how sure you can be.

The Skew d6 is designed and sold by The Dice Lab. The drawing on this page is an original rendering.

The Skew d6 on this page is based on real classroom rolls collected by AP Statistics teacher Doug Tyson and his students.

Your rolls are saved only in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
